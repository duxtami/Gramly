Gramly Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gramly ("the App") handles your information and protects your privacy.

Data Collection and Usage
Gramly is designed to provide calculator and measurement tools directly on your device.
We value your privacy. The App only accesses the information necessary to provide its core features. All processing happens locally on your device, and we do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Information We Access
We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal data to use Gramly. Any data generated or calculations performed within the App are strictly for your own use.
This information is used only to perform calculations and personalize your experience.

Permissions
Gramly does not request any Android runtime permissions.
The App does not require access to:
- Storage
- Photos or Media
- Notifications
- Contacts
- Microphone
- Camera
- Location
- Bluetooth
- Phone

Internet Access
Gramly operates entirely offline. The App does not monitor, collect, or track your internet activity, and no internet connection is required for its core functionality.

Local Data Storage
The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device, including:
- App preferences
- Theme settings
- Calculation history
This data never leaves your device and is only removed if you explicitly choose to clear the app's data or uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services
Gramly operates independently. The App does not include or integrate with:
- Advertisements
- Analytics SDKs
- Tracking services
- User profiling
- Background data collection

Data Sharing
We respect your privacy and do not share your data. Specifically, we do not:
- Sell your personal data
- Share your information with advertisers
- Upload your personal data to any servers
- Collect unnecessary personal information

Security
Reasonable measures are taken to protect your information. Because Gramly operates entirely offline, all of your data and calculation history remain securely isolated on your device.

Children's Privacy
Gramly is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published within the App or on the official project page.
